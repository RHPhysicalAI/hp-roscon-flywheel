Primary metric: success rate (all three cubes on the tray, task_success), including rejected episodes in the denominator when curator verdicts exist. A failed episode whose cube count could not be read, or whose rollout did not finish, is not a verdict on the policy: it is left out of the rate and counted on the card as not scored. Secondary metric: smoothness among successful episodes only — failed episodes often barely move, which would make a worse policy look smoother. Wilson confidence intervals are shown; they describe uncertainty in the observed rate, not proof that one policy is statistically better than another.
